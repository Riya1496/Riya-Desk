What we check

Four places leads leak

We visit your website and public data to find specific gaps costing you leads.

01

Website Tracking

The Problem: Can Google/Facebook track visitors from your ads?

Why It Matters: Without tracking, you can't measure ROI or retarget visitors.

What We Check: Do you have GA4, Meta Pixel, Google Ads tracking installed?

02

AI Readiness

The Problem: More people now ask AI assistants like ChatGPT and Gemini for recommendations. Can they tell who you are, where you are and what you offer?

Why It Matters: AI tools recommend businesses they can clearly identify. Vague websites get skipped.

What We Check: The signals on your website that AI reads: how clearly your name, location, services and business details come through.

03

Contact Form

The Problem: How many fields does your contact form have?

Why It Matters: 1-2 fields = 80% conversion. 8+ fields = 20% conversion.

What We Check: Form length + booking/chat availability on your site.

04

Customer Reviews

The Problem: How many reviews do you have vs competitors?

Why It Matters: More reviews = more trust = more conversions.

What We Check: Your rating + review count vs local competitors.

FAQ

How reliable is this?

Do you actually check my website?

Yes. We fetch your website and read the HTML code (like a search engine crawler would). We look for actual tracking pixels, forms, and contact methods. Not guessing.

How do you score AI readiness?

We read your live website the way an AI assistant would and measure how clearly your name, location, services and business details come through. It shows how ready your site is to be recommended. It isn’t a count of ChatGPT mentions, because AI answers change from person to person.

What if my website is down?

We'll tell you. If your site isn't live when we scan, we can't analyze it. Make sure it's working before you submit.

What do you do with my data?

We save your email to send results + let you contact us. We don't sell data, use it for marketing, or share it. Period.

Is this 100% accurate?

No—it's a diagnostic, not gospel. We check what's publicly available + on your site. Use it to identify gaps, then verify with your own data.

Will you charge me later?

No. The scan is free. You only pay if you hire us to implement fixes. We'll quote you per project based on complexity.
