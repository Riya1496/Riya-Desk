🎯 Product Refinement - Building Trust & Clarity

Redesigned the Lead Leak Scan from a generic tool into a trustworthy diagnostic that wins user confidence.

OBJECTIVE: Get users to sign up & contact us

🔐 Trust Issue #1: "How Are You Fetching This Data?"

Solution: Full Transparency on Methodology

Showed results for non-existent websites with no explanation. Users think: "How do they know this? I didn't give them this data."
NEW Landing Page explains:
  • "We visit your website (like a customer would)"
  • "We read the HTML to find tracking pixels and forms"
  • "We check public sources like Google Business and ChatGPT"
  • "Your results show what we actually found, not estimates"
Added to results page: "Methodology" section showing
  • ✓ Website was verified (live/accessible)
  • ✓ Data sources checked
  • ✓ What we can measure (with confidence)
  • ✓ What we can't measure (honest limitations)

🔐 Trust Issue #2: Website Validation

Solution: Verify Website Exists Before Scanning

No validation. User enters "Sydney Insurance Solutions" (doesn't exist), gets results anyway. Looks unreliable.
NEW Form Flow:
  • User clicks "Get Free Scan"
  • Status: "Verifying website..."
  • If website is down: ❌ "Website not found. Make sure it's live and accessible."
  • If website is live: ✓ Proceed with scan

📖 Clarity Issue #1: Technical Jargon

Solution: Rename to Plain English Terms

OLD (Confusing) NEW (Clear) Why Better
Ad Pixel Audit 📊 Website Tracking Anyone can understand "tracking"
AI Visibility 🤖 ChatGPT Mentions Specific AI tool users know
Lead Capture Friction 📋 Contact Form Everyone knows what a form is
Reputation & Reviews ⭐ Customer Reviews Direct language

📖 Clarity Issue #2: Form Instructions

Solution: Define What We Check & Set Expectations

NEW Landing Page includes:
  • "What We Check" section - 4 boxes explaining each check in plain language:
    • What the problem is
    • Why it matters
    • What we actually check for
  • Form field hints:
    • "Business name: How your business appears (or should appear) on Google"
    • "Website: We'll verify it's live and analyze what's on it"
    • "City: Where customers find you"
    • "Email: We'll send results + next steps"
  • FAQ section answering trust concerns:
    • "How do you check my website?"
    • "Is this 100% accurate?"
    • "Will you charge me later?"
    • "What if my website is down?"

📊 Results Page: Show Findings Clearly

Dynamic 2-3 Line Explanations Based on ACTUAL Data

Example outputs (each one is unique to their website):
  • Website Tracking: "We found: 0 of 7 pixels. We found: No tracking pixels. Impact: Can't measure which ads convert. Can't retarget visitors."
  • ChatGPT Mentions: "We found: 0 mentions when asking ChatGPT. Issue: 46% of market asks AI before Google."
  • Contact Form: "We found: No contact form. Problem: Visitors can't easily reach you."
  • Customer Reviews: "We found: 5 reviews at 4.2★. Action: Collect more reviews. Builds trust & SEO."
NOT hardcoded mock findings. Each result is tailored to their website scan.
