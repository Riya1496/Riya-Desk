03 — Problems & solutions

Six problems. Six fixes.

Purpose Turn the audit into clear changes the team can start this month.

Problem 01

Views don’t turn into followers

About 10M views, 3,463 followers. Viewers enjoy the Reel and scroll on.

Solution

Brand every Reel, and give a reason to follow

A consistent end card (logo + “Follow for real shaadi moments”), a visible watermark, and a pinned comment pointing to the booking highlight.

Problem 02

The logo doesn’t work small

Watercolour, leaves, a frame, rings and a tiny “by Snapbyte” all fight in a 110px circle, and the watermark is unreadable on Reels.

Solution

A simpler wordmark and a monogram

Keep the name and the rings, drop the rest. A WL monogram for the profile picture and watermark (see section 06).

Problem 03

No consistent look

Pastel logo, velvet highlights, beige posts, neon Reel text. The feed doesn’t feel like one studio.

Solution

Build on what’s already best: velvet maroon + ivory

The highlight covers and editorial posts are the strongest visuals. Make them the system: one palette, one serif, one Reel text style.

Problem 04

Two brand names

Wedding Lensation and Snapbyte both appear, so trust and search split between them.

Solution

Lead with Wedding Lensation

Use Wedding Lensation everywhere couples look. Keep Snapbyte as the company name on invoices and the website footer (“A Snapbyte studio”), not in the logo.

Problem 05

The real differentiators are hidden

12 years, 500+ weddings, Mumbai + Jabalpur and destination weddings in five countries are buried in emoji.

Solution

Say it plainly, everywhere

A rewritten bio, a “Destination” highlight and a regular proof post (“Wedding no. 501”).

Problem 06

No path from “wow” to booking

Highlights named by initials, no packages or process, a long tracking link.

Solution

A clear booking path

Highlights renamed (Book a date, Films, Brides, Real Shaadi, Destination, Reviews), a clean link, and a “Check your date” call to action on every post.

04 — Positioning

Editorial frames. Real shaadi.

Purpose Name the one thing Wedding Lensation already does better than local studios, then build everything around it.

What the couple wants

Magazine-worthy portraits.

The “Vogue” bride, “The groom”: polished, editorial frames to frame on the wall.

What the family remembers

The real shaadi.

Dads sounding like Jethalal, the bride saying “no more photos”, the phone-torch moment. Their Reels prove people love this.

Most studios pick one. Wedding Lensation does both.

For families in Central India and couples planning weddings in Mumbai or abroad, Wedding Lensation is the 12-year photography and film team that delivers editorial portraits and the real, funny, emotional shaadi, all in one story.

Audience 01

Jabalpur & Central India families

The core market. They value experience, every ritual covered, and a team they can trust. Proof: 12 years, 500+ weddings.

Audience 02

Mumbai couples

Instagram-first, want editorial style and Reels they can share. Proof: the views and the editorial grid.

Audience 03

Destination weddings

Higher budgets, need a team that has done it before abroad. Proof: Thailand, Singapore, Vietnam, Malaysia, UAE.

Audiences are drawn from the locations and work on the profile. Validate with where actual enquiries come from.

10 — Roadmap

First 90 days.

Purpose Put the changes in order, so the quick wins come first.

Month 1

Fix the profile

  • New bio, name field and clean link
  • Rename highlights and design new covers
  • Pin three posts
  • Finalise the new logo and monogram
Month 2

Make Reels recognisable

  • One Reel title style and end card
  • Visible watermark on every video
  • Content mix: 40 / 30 / 20 / 10
  • Pinned “check your date” comment
Month 3

Measure what matters

  • Track follows and DMs per Reel, not just views
  • Ask every enquiry “How did you find us?”
  • Launch a Destination highlight and post series
  • Review and adjust the mix
