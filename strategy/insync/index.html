01 — Account audit

What Insync has, and what’s holding it back.

Purpose Know exactly where we’re starting, so every later piece fixes a real gap instead of guessing.

Strengths to build on

  • A sharp niche. Marathi couple humour (नवरा / बायको, शांत नवरा / भांडखोर बायको) is specific enough to feel like it was made for you.
  • Every product is a pair. Twelve designs in six matching pairs give a built-in reason to buy two, not one.
  • Made for sharing. The jokes work as content on their own. People tag their partner before they ever think about buying.
  • A recognisable identity. The bubbly green-and-coral logo is warm, playful and easy to repeat across every post.

Gaps to fix first

  • Flat mock-ups only. The current product images are t-shirt mock-ups. Real couples wearing them would show fit, colour and, above all, the joke landing.
  • Mixed colours across designs. Maroon, black, red, teal and cream sit side by side. Grouping them into named pairs on the grid would make the feed feel planned.
  • Marathi-only punchlines. They’re the charm, but a short English line in captions lets gifting friends and non-Marathi partners in on the joke too.
  • No clear path to buy. Every post needs one obvious next step: link in bio, a pinned “Shop the pairs” post, or a DM keyword.

02 — Content pillars

Four kinds of post, each with one job.

Purpose Keep the feed varied without being random. Every post fits a pillar, and every pillar moves people one step closer to buying.

40%

Inside jokes

Relatable couple moments built on the designs: the fights, the “तू म्हणशील तसं”, the डोकेदुखी.

Job: reach & shares
30%

Pair it up

The products, always shown as matching pairs, with who each one is for and how they look together.

Job: consideration & saves
20%

Gift it

Anniversaries, birthdays, Valentine’s, Diwali and weddings: ready-made answers for “what do I get them?”

Job: sales
10%

Behind the print

Packing orders, handwritten notes, new designs in progress: the small brand behind the jokes.

Job: trust
