What “qualified” means for you

Agreed with Northline on Oct 20

  • Needs home, auto, or both
  • Lives in a state where you’re licensed
  • Current policy renews within 60 days, or has no cover
  • Agreed to be contacted, with consent recorded

This definition is what Google and Meta are told to look for. Change it, and the ads change who they find.
